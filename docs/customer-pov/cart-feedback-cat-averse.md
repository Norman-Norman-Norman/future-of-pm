# Cart Evaluation — Frank Bissette, Bissette Commercial Cleaning Services

**Date:** March 10, 2026
**Reviewer:** Frank Bissette, Owner — Bissette Commercial Cleaning Services, Raleigh, NC
**Account Size:** $1,200–$4,000/order, weekly standing orders
**Evaluation Context:** Reviewing the OctoCAT Supply shopping cart at 6:30 AM, desktop, reading glasses on, spiral notebook open.

---

## 1. Overall Rating: 3/10

I've been buying industrial cleaning supplies for 22 years. I've used Grainger, Uline, Zoro, a dozen regional distributors. This cart is the most unprofessional ordering experience I've encountered from a company that expects me to spend real money.

The cart layout itself is *fine*. It's a table. It has columns. I can see what I'm buying. But everything surrounding it — the branding, the product catalog, the lack of basic commercial purchasing features — tells me this company doesn't take B2B customers seriously.

---

## 2. Professionalism

**Rating: 2/10**

- The navigation bar says **"OctoCAT Supply — Smart Cat Tech, Powered by AI."** That's the tagline. *Smart Cat Tech.* I'm buying floor stripper and 55-gallon drums of degreaser. This tagline would make my client at Duke Medical question whether I'm sourcing from a real supplier.
- The footer says: *"OctoCAT Supply is the leading provider of AI-powered smart products for your feline companions."* For your *feline companions*. I showed this to my office manager and she asked if I was ordering cat toys by mistake.
- No phone number. Anywhere. Not in the header, not in the footer, not on the cart page. When I have a billing discrepancy at 7 AM and my crews are loading the trucks, I need a phone number. I don't need a chatbot. I don't need a ticket form. I need to call someone.
- The "Help Center" link in the footer goes nowhere — it's a dead link (points to #). Same for "My Cart," "Checkout," "Shopping Details," and "Order" in the Account section. That's five dead links in the footer of a business that wants my money.
- No physical address listed. No company registration. Nothing that says "we are a real, licensed, insured supply company."

## 4. Functional Assessment

**Ignoring the branding, how does the cart actually work?**

**What works:**
- Clean table layout with Serial Number, Product Image, Product Name, Unit Price, Quantity, Total, and Remove columns. This is a sensible structure.
- Editable quantity field (number input). I can type a quantity directly — that's good for bulk orders.
- Remove button (trash icon) per line item. Clear and functional.
- Coupon code field with "Apply Coupon" button. Useful if your promo codes work (the "TechConnect" code from the popup presumably applies here).
- Order Summary sidebar with Subtotal, Discount, Shipping, and Grand Total. Basic but functional.
- "Update Cart" button to recalculate after quantity changes.
- Cart badge on the navigation icon showing item count (green badge with number). Helpful.

**What's broken or missing:**
- **No PO (Purchase Order) number field.** Every B2B order I place has a PO number. My clients require it for reconciliation. Without a PO field, I can't match this order to my internal tracking.
- **No invoice preview or download.** I need to see what my invoice will look like BEFORE I commit to checkout. If it has a cartoon cat on it, I need to know now so I can request a clean version.
- **No SDS (Safety Data Sheet) access.** If I'm ordering chemical products (which I would be if this sold cleaning supplies), I need one-click SDS access from the product listing or cart.
- **No tax calculation.** The cart shows Subtotal, a 5% discount, and $10 flat shipping. No sales tax. For a $400+ order, I need to know my tax liability before proceeding.
- **Flat $10 shipping is suspicious.** $10 to ship multiple items? What's the actual shipping cost? Is it weight-based? Does it change for hazmat chemicals? Flat $10 suggests this hasn't been thought through for commercial quantities.
- **Automatic 5% discount with no explanation.** Where did this 5% come from? Is it a loyalty discount? A volume discount? A promotional rate? My spiral notebook needs a reason for every price adjustment.
- **Unit prices are rounded to whole dollars.** The cart shows "$130" not "$129.99." When I'm reconciling invoices across 4 suppliers, I need the exact price to the penny. Rounding to the dollar loses precision.
- **Cart is not persistent.** If I navigate away and come back, or if I refresh the page, the cart is EMPTY. It's stored in browser memory only. I place orders over 15-20 minutes while cross-referencing my supply closet inventory. A cart that disappears on page refresh is unusable for my workflow.
- **No "Save for Later" or wishlist.** I compare prices quarterly. I need to save potential orders while I check competitor pricing.
- **No bulk pricing or quantity breaks.** I buy 30-gallon trash liners in cases of 250. There should be tier pricing for volume.
- **"Proceed to Checkout" button is a gradient pill-shaped button.** It works, but the rounded-full pill shape with the green gradient looks more like a gaming app than a commercial purchasing system. A standard rectangular button would be more professional.
- **No payment method selection.** Net-30? Credit card? ACH? I need net terms for commercial accounts. Consumers pay on checkout. Businesses need invoicing.

## 5. Would You Buy?

**No.** Not from this cart, and not from this site.

The cart mechanics are adequate for a consumer shopping experience. Table layout, quantity editing, order summary — that's the bare minimum and they have it. But "adequate for consumer" is not "acceptable for commercial."

I spend $50,000–$100,000 annually with my primary supplier. To earn that business, a supplier needs:
- A real person I can call
- PO number support
- Net-30 terms
- Professional invoices I can forward to my clients
- Price-to-the-penny accuracy
- Current SDS for every chemical I buy
- A cart that doesn't forget what I put in it

This site has none of those things. The prices might be 11% cheaper, but cheap doesn't matter if I can't produce a clean invoice for my client at Duke Medical without a cartoon animal on it.

## 7. Missing Professional Features

In order of importance for a commercial buyer like me:

1. **Phone number on every page** — I need to call someone when something goes wrong. This is non-negotiable.
2. **PO number field on the cart/checkout page** — Every order I place has a PO number for my internal tracking and client billing.
3. **Persistent cart** — Store cart contents in a user account or at minimum localStorage. A cart that evaporates on page refresh is broken.
4. **Professional invoice template** — No mascot. No tagline. Just: company name, address, items, prices, taxes, total, PO number, and payment terms.
5. **Net-30 / payment terms** — Commercial accounts need invoicing, not just credit card checkout.
6. **Tax calculation** — Show me the sales tax before I check out.
7. **SDS access** — One click from the product page or cart to the Safety Data Sheet. For medical and warehouse contracts, this is legally required.
8. **Account rep / dedicated contact** — Give me a named person who knows my account. Put their name, email, and direct phone number on my account page.
9. **Order history and reorder** — I buy the same products every week. Let me reorder from history in 2 clicks.
10. **Precise pricing** — Show me $129.99, not $130. Every penny matters when I'm comparing across suppliers.
11. **Actual product catalog** — Where are the cleaning chemicals, trash liners, paper products, and PPE? All I see are AI-powered cat toys.

---

## 8. Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. Add a phone number to the header and footer — today.

Not tomorrow. Not "in the next sprint." Today. Put a real phone number where a customer can reach a real person. I've been buying supplies for 22 years and the single biggest differentiator between a supplier I keep and a supplier I drop is whether I can get a human on the phone when something goes wrong. Chatbots don't solve "you shipped the wrong product and my crew is at the job site in 2 hours."

### 2. Build a "Professional Mode" or B2B account experience.

Strip the cat branding from invoices, order confirmations, and account pages. Add PO number support. Add net-30 terms for approved commercial accounts. Show exact pricing (to the cent). Provide SDS access. Make the cart persistent. In short: let me use this site like a supply company, not a pet boutique.

### 3. Kill the promo popup or limit it to first-time visitors.

I have never, in 22 years, placed an order because a popup told me to. The "EXCLUSIVE SALE! GitHub Copilot Chef's Hat!" modal blocks the entire product page. If I'm a repeat customer trying to reorder at 6:30 AM, that popup is the single fastest way to make me close the tab and call Grainger instead. If you must have promos, put them in a banner. Don't block my workflow.
