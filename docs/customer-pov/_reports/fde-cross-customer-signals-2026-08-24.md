# FDE cross-customer signals - 2026-08-24

Internal working notes. This repository is public; do not commit or publish confidential customer details without review.

## Strongest recurring product signal

The strongest cross-customer pattern in the current triage is enterprise control and visibility for AI-assisted development:

- billing and cost-center attribution
- usage and token transparency
- model entitlement and feature-toggle governance
- MCP and agent governance
- centralized policy management
- enterprise telemetry and reporting

## Why it matters

These asks cut across individual customer engagements. They are not only customer-success requests; they affect product positioning against competing tools, customer trust in agentic workflows, and the ability of enterprise administrators to expand usage safely.

## Recommended product escalation

| Audience | Evidence package should include |
|---|---|
| Enterprise PMs | Admin controls, entitlement model, feature toggles, policy requirements |
| Governance PMs | MCP/agent governance, approval paths, centralized policy needs |
| Metrics/Analytics PMs | Cost-center attribution, token visibility, usage reporting, budget controls |

## Next step

Build one sanitized evidence package that groups examples by product capability rather than by customer name, then review it before sharing outside the FDE/customer-response team.
