# Pricing & Value Features Feedback — Carmen Delgado, Budget-Conscious Buyer

**Reviewer:** Carmen Delgado, Office Manager, Phoenix AZ Nonprofit (youth education, 35 staff)  
**Date:** March 10, 2026  
**Pages Tested:** Product Catalog (`/products`), Shopping Cart (`/cart`), Product Detail Modal  
**Device:** Chromebook (desktop browser)  
**Scenario:** First-Tuesday-of-the-month ordering routine — evaluating whether OctoCAT Supply can replace Amazon Business as our primary cat supply vendor. $8,000 annual budget, board reviews every line item.

---

## Overall Rating: 3/10 — Cannot justify switching from Amazon Business

I spent 45 minutes comparing OctoCAT Supply to what I get from Amazon Business. I had my spreadsheet open, 4 supplier tabs ready to go. The products are excellent — Grant (our therapy cat) would love half this catalog. But the pricing infrastructure is so incomplete that I cannot defend a single purchase to my board. I'm not buying from a site that makes me do more math than my accountant.

---

## 1. Price Clarity — FAILING

### No Per-Unit Pricing

Every product is sold as "piece" but there's no per-unit breakdown visible anywhere. The product cards show a price — $129.99, $79.99 — but what am I comparing to? If Amazon shows me a similar smart feeder at $119.99 with free Prime shipping, OctoCAT's price needs to be defensible. Right now, the only way I know the unit is by reading the raw data model (`unit: "piece"`). That doesn't show up on the product card or in the cart.

**What I need on every product card:**
- Price per unit ($/piece, $/case, $/oz — whatever applies)
- Pack size if bundled
- "Price per unit" label so I can screenshot it for my spreadsheet

**What Amazon Business shows me:** Per-unit price on every listing. Always. I sort by per-unit price. That's how I make decisions.

### Prices Truncated in Cart

I covered this in my cart feedback, but it bears repeating: the cart rounds $129.99 to $130. That's $0.01 I can't account for. Over a year of orders, those pennies add up — and more importantly, they make my receipts not match the product catalog. My board will ask. They always ask.

### No Tax Estimate

I see subtotal, a mysterious 5% discount, $10 shipping, and grand total. Where's sales tax? In Arizona, I'm looking at 5.6% state + local. On a $600 order, that's $33.60 I need to budget for. Hiding it until checkout (which doesn't work anyway) means I can't plan my order within budget.

---

## 2. Discount Visibility — BROKEN AND INCOMPLETE

### The 25% Discount Display

Four products show a "25% OFF" badge on the product page. The visual is actually good — the crossed-out original price with the sale price in green is exactly what I want to see. SmartFeeder One: ~~$129.99~~ → **$97.49**. That's clear. That's what I'd put in my spreadsheet.

But as I documented in my cart feedback, the cart charges FULL PRICE. This isn't a display issue — it's a billing issue. It disqualifies this supplier immediately.

### No Filter for Discounted Items

I have 13 products on screen. 4 are discounted. I can't filter to show ONLY discounted items. On my budget, I start with sale items. Every time. Amazon Business lets me check "Deals & Savings" in the sidebar. Here I have to scroll through the whole catalog, squinting at tiny discount badges.

**What I need:** A filter toggle — "Show discounted items only" or "On Sale" — or even just a "Sort by: Discount" option. On the first Tuesday of the month when I'm building my order, I need the cheapest options front and center.

### No Discount Tiers / Volume Pricing

There is zero volume pricing. I buy SmartFeeders for our 3 program rooms. That's 3 units. Does buying 3 get me anything? 5? 10? Nothing. No tiers, no "buy more save more," no nothing.

**What Amazon Business gives me:** Business Pricing on most items. "1 unit: $129.99 | 3+ units: $119.99 | 10+ units: $109.99." I can show my ED: "If we buy 5 now, we save $50. That's next month's coffee budget."

**The calculation I can't make:** Without volume tiers, I can't build a case for buying in bulk. I can't show my board a savings projection. I just have a flat price and a prayer.

### The "techconnect" Coupon — Undiscoverable

There is exactly one coupon code in this system: "techconnect" for 5% off. How would I know this? How would ANY customer know this? There is no:
- Banner on the homepage saying "Use code TECHCONNECT for 5% off!"
- Email signup offering a first-order discount code
- "Available coupons" section in the cart
- Coupon in the product detail modal

I only know this code exists because it was in the popup on the product page — and even then, the popup says "30% OFF GitHub Copilot Chef's Hat" with a promo code, but the actual coupon gives a blanket 5% off everything. Mixed signals. Confusing.

**And as I covered in my cart feedback — the coupon doesn't actually change the total anyway.** It says "Applied!" and does nothing. So it's both undiscoverable AND broken.

## 3. Comparison Support — NONEXISTENT

### No Side-by-Side Comparison

I want to compare the PawTrack Smart Collar ($79.99) with the ZoomieTracker AI Mat ($79.99). Same price. Which one gives Grant more value? I need to see them next to each other: features, specs, price, and ideally reviews. Currently, I have to click one, read the modal, close it, click the other, read that modal, try to remember what the first one said.

**What I need:** Checkboxes on product cards → "Compare" button → side-by-side table showing name, price, unit cost, description, discount, and eventually ratings.

**What Amazon does:** "Compare with similar items" table right on the product page. Price, ratings, features, Prime eligibility — all in one view.

### No Wishlist / Save for Later

I can't save items to compare later. My ordering process takes days — I browse on Monday, compare prices Tuesday, order Wednesday after Linda approves. There's no way to bookmark products on this site. No wishlist. No "save for later." No favorites.

If I close my Chromebook tab, my entire research session is gone. The cart doesn't persist either (covered in cart feedback). This means I have to start from scratch every session.

### No Price History

Has the SmartFeeder ever been cheaper than $97.49 (after 25% off)? Will it go on sale next month? I have no idea. Amazon shows price history via CamelCamelCamel. Even Chewy shows "Was $X, now $Y." OctoCAT shows me one price and that's it.

## 6. Loyalty & Rewards — Missing the Obvious

### The Paw Points Opportunity

I order twice a month, every month. That's 24 orders a year. If OctoCAT had a loyalty program — even a simple one like "5% back in points on every order" — I'd have a financial incentive to consolidate my purchasing here. Right now, I split orders across 4 suppliers based on whoever has the best price that day. Give me a reason to stay.

**What would make me a loyal customer:**
- Points per dollar spent (redeemable for discounts)
- Spend-tier pricing ("Silver: 5% off all orders, Gold: 10% off after $2,000/year")
- Free shipping after spending $X per month
- Early access to sales

### No Subscription / Auto-Reorder

Grant eats the same food every month. We use the same office supplies every month. I would LOVE to set up "auto-ship SmartFeeder refills every 30 days at 15% off." Amazon Subscribe & Save has trained me to expect this. It saves me time AND money. OctoCAT doesn't have it.

## 8. Top 5 Recommendations — Ranked by Budget Impact

### 1. FIX DISCOUNTED PRICES IN CART (Showstopper)
Products with 25% off MUST reflect the discounted price in the cart. Currently the cart charges $129.99 for something advertised at $97.49. This is an overcharge. At my ordering volume, this bug would cost my nonprofit hundreds of dollars per year. I will not place a single order until this is fixed.

### 2. ADD EXPORT: CSV/PDF ORDER SUMMARY
After I build a cart (or place an order), I need a "Download as CSV" button that gives me: Date, Product Name, SKU, Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Line Total, Subtotal, Shipping, Tax, Grand Total. This is the single feature that would let me include OctoCAT Supply in my board packet.

### 3. SHOW PER-UNIT PRICING ON PRODUCT CARDS AND IN CART
Every product card should display "$/piece" (or appropriate unit). The cart should show unit price to the cent ($129.99, not $130). Without this, I can't build my comparison spreadsheet, and comparison shopping is how I justify every purchase.

### 4. IMPLEMENT VOLUME DISCOUNT TIERS
Show pricing tiers on the product page: "1-4 units: $129.99 | 5-9 units: $116.99 (10% off) | 10+: $110.49 (15% off)." This gives me a reason to consolidate orders here instead of splitting across suppliers. And it gives me a savings story for my board.

### 5. FREE SHIPPING THRESHOLD WITH PROGRESS BAR
"Spend $200+ for free shipping! You're $47 away." This directly changes my ordering behavior. I will add items to hit the threshold. I do this on every other supplier site. It saves me money. It increases your order size. Everyone wins.
