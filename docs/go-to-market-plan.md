## 1. Brand Strategy & A/B Testing

### Working Name: "McAfee Sells Cats"
Fun, provocative, gets attention — but lacks gravitas for B2B enterprise buyers. Too meme-able for procurement officers and operations directors to put on a PO.

### A/B Test Candidates

| # | Brand Name | Tagline | Strengths | Weaknesses | Score |
|---|-----------|---------|-----------|------------|-------|
| A | **McAfee Sells Cats** | "Total Feline Protection" | Funny, memorable, viral potential | Not taken seriously in B2B, trademark risk | 5/10 |
| B | **CATAPULT Supply** | "Launch Your Supply Chain Forward" | Action-oriented, professional, implies growth & momentum. Cat is embedded subtly. Works for B2B and B2C. | Less immediately "cat-themed" | **9/10** |
| C | **PawShield** | "Enterprise-Grade Protection for Everything Feline" | McAfee security DNA, strong imagery | Too defensive/narrow, sounds like antivirus for cats | 6/10 |
| D | **NineLives Supply** | "Built to Last. Again and Again." | Memorable, reliability messaging | Slightly playful, may not feel tech-forward | 7/10 |
| E | **CLAWMARK** | "Leave Your Mark on the Supply Chain" | Bold, edgy, brandable | Slightly aggressive for B2B procurement | 7/10 |
| F | **PROWL** | "Intelligent Supply. Predatory Precision." | Short, punchy, tech-forward | Too predatory for a friendly cat brand | 6/10 |

### **Recommendation: CATAPULT Supply**

**Why CATAPULT wins:**
- The word "cat" is embedded organically — **CAT**apult
- "Catapult" means to launch forward with force — perfect for a growth-stage supply platform
- Works in enterprise conversations: *"We source through Catapult"*
- Works in marketing: *"Catapult your supply chain into the future"*
- McAfee-level boldness without the trademark baggage
- Scales from cat tech to general supply chain if the business expands

**Visual Identity (McAfee-inspired):**
- **Primary**: Electric green `#76B852` → upgraded to `#00E676` (bolder, more neon)
- **Dark base**: Pure black `#0A0A0A` with green glows
- **Typography**: Bold, all-caps headlines. Inter Black weight.
- **Icon**: Stylized shield with cat silhouette / launch arrow
- **Feel**: Dark mode first. Confident. Premium. Like a cybersecurity dashboard meets luxury e-commerce.

## 9. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Brand confusion with McAfee | Medium | High | Distinct visual identity, no security language overlap |
| B2B buyers don't take cat branding seriously | Medium | High | Professional sub-brand for enterprise ("Catapult Enterprise") |
| Slow enterprise sales cycle | High | Medium | Self-serve tier to capture SMB while enterprise pipeline builds |
| Competitor response (Chewy enters B2B) | Low | High | Deepen AI/smart-tech moat — no competitor has IoT integration |
| Supply chain for smart products | Medium | High | Dual-source all SKUs, maintain 90-day safety stock |
